# Touchdown Manual

This is the final part of your Advanced Spacecraft Handling Test. Captain Flash is beside you. Bring a two-man landing craft down onto the Moon, three times, touching down almost without speed.

## Controls

- `A`, `Up` or `W`: more thrust.
- `D`, `Down` or `S`: less thrust.
- `Enter`: begin each landing, and move on afterwards.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## Flying

Holding a key moves the thrust lever steadily, not instantly, so think ahead. Gravity pulls you down all the time.

Your thrust can never be higher than the fuel you have left. The orange mark on the thrust gauge shows that ceiling, and it falls as you burn fuel. You cannot use full power for long, so do not spend it too early.

Too much thrust and you start climbing. Climb above where you started and you are lost in space.

## Passing

Touch down at under 4. Under 2 is smooth; under 1 and Captain Flash will barely feel it. All three landings must be safe to pass.
