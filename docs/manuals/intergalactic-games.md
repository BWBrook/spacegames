# Intergalactic Games Manual

New Century TV needs exclusive coverage of the First Intergalactic Games. Your job is to launch the broadcast satellite to the requested height before rival networks seize the signal.

## Controls

- `Left/Right` or `A/D`: adjust launch angle.
- `Up/Down` or `W/S`: adjust launch speed.
- `Shift`: hold while adjusting for fine control.
- `Enter` or `Space`: launch from the aim phase.
- `Space`: during flight, press as the cursor crosses the centre of the broadcast-lock meter.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology menu.

## How To Win

Each contract gives a target height. Tune angle and speed, then launch. You have eight attempts. After each miss, the debrief tells you whether the launch was too shallow, too steep, too slow, or too fast.

The original BASIC win condition is preserved: angle must be within 2 degrees and speed must be within 100 units of the hidden solution.

## Scoring

A successful launch awards the original credit bonus: `1000 / attempt`. Cleaner broadcast lock and fewer mistakes add to the modern score.
