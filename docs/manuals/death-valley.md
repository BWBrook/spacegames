# Death Valley Manual

There is only one way to escape the Dissectitrons: fly your single-seater Speed Dart down Death Valley, a jagged, bottomless ravine, without touching the walls.

## Controls

- `Left` / `Right` before flying: choose the width of the valley.
- `Enter`: start, and after a run, go again.
- `Q` / `P`, or `Left` / `Right`: steer left and right.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## Flying

The valley winds unpredictably and your headlights only reach a short way ahead. Touch a wall and the Dart disintegrates. Survive 200 rows and you are through.

The valley is the same width all the way down. The number you choose sets how much clear air you have. Try 15 first and work your way down. 8 is quite difficult.

The Dart speeds up as you go deeper.

## Scoring

You score for every row you survive, more for narrower valleys, and a bonus for getting all the way through.
