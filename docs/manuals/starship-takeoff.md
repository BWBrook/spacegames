# Starship Take-Off Manual

Reach orbital altitude without overheating the captured ship or breaking its hull.

## Controls

- Prep phase: drag the controls, or use `A/D` for booster power, `W/S` for vector bias, and `Q/E` for fuel mix.
- Commit phase: press `Enter` or `Space` when the ignition cursor is inside the golden gate.
- Ascent phase: use `W/S` or `Up/Down` to adjust thrust. Hold `Space` or `Shift` to vent heat.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology menu.

## How To Win

In prep, match `Projected burn` to the noisy `Telemetry target`, then launch. During ascent, keep thrust near the hidden target while managing heat and events. Reach 32,000 m with hull integrity above zero and heat below the fail-safe limit.

## HUD And Replay

The mission panel shows telemetry, burn offset, altitude, hull, heat, and the next event. The right slider controls live thrust; the timeline previews upcoming incidents. Press `Enter` after debrief to roll a new target and try for a cleaner flight.
