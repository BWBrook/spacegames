# Monsters of Galacticon Manual

Landing was easy. Nobody mentioned the monsters. Eight of them will come at you, and there are five in your group.

## Controls

- `R`: ray gun.
- `S`: sword laser.
- `T`: trypton blaster.
- `Enter` after a run: land again.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## What Happens

Each monster answers to exactly one of the three weapons. Use it and the monster dies.

Of the other two, one does nothing, though there is a fair chance of angering the monster into taking one of your group. The other is no use at all and costs you a member for certain. Lose all five and the expedition is over.

The names are shuffled every time you land, so what worked last run tells you nothing this run.

## The Field Journal

The panel on the right fills in as you learn. It records only what you actually saw.

Look at it carefully. The three outcomes are not scattered at random across the weapons, and the debrief at the end of a run lists what every monster needed. A few runs of reading that table will tell you something that makes this game a great deal easier.
