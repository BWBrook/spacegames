# Trip Into The Future Manual

A ship moving close to the speed of light keeps slower time than the Earth it left. Fly fast enough and you can come home decades in Earth's future having aged only a few years yourself. Fly too slowly and you will die of old age on the way.

## Controls

- `Up` / `Down`: set your speed.
- `Left` / `Right`: set the length of your trip.
- Hold `Shift` with either for fine adjustment.
- `Enter`: launch, and after a trip, go on to the next mission.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## Each Mission

Your orders say how many years must pass on Earth before you return. You set out aged 30 and will not live past 80, so you have 50 years to spend aboard.

Your engines have a top speed for each mission. The panel shows how much faster Earth's clock runs than yours at the speed you have chosen: "1 year aboard = 2.29 years on Earth" means that for every year you age, Earth ages 2.29.

## Working It Out

The time that passes on Earth is simply the length of the trip divided by your speed. A trip of 90 light years at 0.9 of light speed takes 100 years as Earth counts them.

The time that passes for you is Earth's time divided by the dilation figure. At 0.9 of light speed that is 100 divided by 2.29, about 44 years. You would come home aged 74, on time.

Arrive within 5 years of the target and you are on time. More than 50 years aboard and you do not arrive at all.

There are five missions in a run, and they get harder.
