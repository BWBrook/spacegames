# Space Mines Manual

You are the newly elected leader of a mining colony on the planet Astron, for ten years. You decide how much ore to sell to the Intergalactic Traders, how much food to buy, and whether to buy or sell mines.

## Controls

- `Up` / `Down`: choose which order to change.
- Type a number, or use `Left` / `Right` to change it. Hold `Shift` for big steps. `Backspace` corrects a typed number.
- `Enter`: confirm the year's orders, and move on from the year's report.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## Each Year

Your mines produce ore at the start of the year. Prices for ore and for mines change every year. Sell ore when the price is good; it keeps in the store if it is not.

## Keeping Them Happy

Food is set in dollars a head. Under $80 a head and satisfaction falls. Over $120 and it rises. Anything in between leaves it where it is.

Above 1.1, the colony grows and the mines get more productive. Below 0.9, it shrinks and they decline. Below 0.6, the people revolt.

## Don't Overwork Them

Each mine needs at least ten people. Fewer than that and they walk out. Some colonies start with too many mines for their people, so check at once.

## Losing

The people revolt, you overwork them, or fewer than thirty people are left. Radioactive leaks happen, rarely, and a mine that becomes too productive floods the market.

## Scoring

Survive your ten years to score. The more people Astron has when you leave, the better your place in its history.
