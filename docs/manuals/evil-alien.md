# Evil Alien Manual

Elron, the Evil Alien, is cloaked somewhere in the 10 by 10 by 10 sector beneath your ship. You have four space bombs, and he is creeping closer while you hunt.

## Controls

- `Arrow keys`: move the target cell on the sector scan. X runs east to the right, Y runs north to the top.
- `W` / `S`: move the distance marker nearer or further. Distance 0 is just below your ship, 9 is the deepest band.
- `Enter` or `Space`: drop a bomb at the chosen cell and distance.
- `Enter` or `Space` after a debrief: start a new hunt.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology menu.

## How To Win

A bomb destroys Elron only when all three numbers match. After a miss, the scanner log reports which way Elron lies from your shot: `NORTH` or `SOUTH`, `EAST` or `WEST`, and `TOO FAR` or `NOT FAR ENOUGH`. The scan shades out every row, column, and distance band those words rule out, and the log footer shows what remains possible on each axis.

Four bombs is enough: split the remaining range on each axis with every shot and the fourth bomb lands.

## Proximity

The gauge at the top right fills as Elron closes in. Each bomb you drop speeds him up. When it fills, he captures you and the hunt is lost, so do not linger.

## Scoring

A hit scores 1000, plus 500 for every bomb left in the rack, plus up to 1000 for a low proximity reading. Losses score nothing.
