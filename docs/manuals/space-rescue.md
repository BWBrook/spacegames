# Space Rescue Manual

A developing planet across the spiral arm of the Galaxy needs medical supplies. The journey is so long that you sleep through it, so before you go under you must program the ship: how much energy for the engines, for life support, and for the shields. Whatever is left is all you will have for the landing.

## Controls

- `Up` / `Down`: choose a field.
- Type a number, or use `Left` / `Right` to change it. `Shift` makes the steps bigger. `Backspace` corrects a typed number.
- `Enter`: launch, commit the landing, and move on from a report.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## Planning the Voyage

- **Engines** set your speed. Speed comes in whole units and each costs a perfect square of energy: 100 buys speed 10, 121 speed 11. On the engine field, Left and Right step one speed at a time.
- **Life support** is worn away by X-ray bursts, 21 to 60 each. If it reaches zero you die.
- **Shields** are worn away by asteroid storms, 21 to 60 each. If they go below zero you are blown up.
- Computer breakdowns add distance, and engine trouble costs half a unit of speed.

You must arrive within the time limit. The panel shows your chances of each, and of reaching orbit, for the plan you have set.

## Landing

In orbit you learn whether gravity and the atmosphere are low, medium or high. Boosters must cover the gravity, heat shields the atmosphere; the panel says how much of each band your figures cover. You also need more than 25 units left over, or you will land safely and be unable to open the door.

## Promotion

There are three missions. Rescue all three and you are made Space Admiral.
