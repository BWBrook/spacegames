# Asteroid Belt Manual

You are flying through the Asteroid Belt. To get through, you have to destroy each asteroid, and the force it takes depends on its size.

## Controls

- `1` to `9`: fire at that strength.
- `Enter` after a run: fly the belt again.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## How To Play

Each asteroid is a cluster of rocks. Count them and fire with the same number. You have a little over a second, and you get one shot.

Too little force and the shot bounces off. Too much and the asteroid shatters, and the pieces hit you anyway. Too slow and you fly straight into it.

The rocks always come in rows of three. Count rows, not rocks.

## Scoring

100 for each asteroid destroyed, more for destroying it quickly, and a bonus for your longest run of hits. The log at the end tells you whether you tend to count too high or too low.
