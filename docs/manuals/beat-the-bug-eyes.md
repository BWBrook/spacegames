# Beat The Bug Eyes Manual

You are hemmed in. Space bugs peer out from behind four rock outcrops, one at a time, and slither back if you are slow. You have a proton blaster and there are ten of them.

## Controls

- `1` `2` `3` `4`: blast the matching outcrop. The number is shown on a badge beneath each rock.
- `Enter` after a debrief: face them again.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## How To Play

Watch the rocks. When a pair of eyes appears, press that rock's number before the ring around you runs out. You get one shot per bug, so a wrong number misses just as surely as being too slow, and firing before the eyes show wastes the shot entirely.

The window starts at a second and a half and tightens as the run goes on.

## Being Hemmed In

Every bug you fail to blast edges its outcrop closer, marked by the pips under the badge. Three misses at the same rock and that bug reaches you, ending the run however many you blasted. Blasting a bug at a rock that has crept forward drives it back one step.

## Scoring

100 for each bug blasted, up to 120 more for each one blasted quickly, and 500 for surviving all ten. Your fastest and average reaction times are shown at the end.
