# Moonlander Manual

You are bringing a lunar module and its crew down to the surface. Slowing the descent costs fuel, and you have 120 units of it.

## Controls

- `Up` or `W`: open the throttle.
- `Down` or `S`: close the throttle.
- `Enter` after a landing: fly again.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## The Numbers

You start 500 units up, falling at 50, with 120 fuel. Gravity adds 5 to your speed every second. The throttle takes that much away again, up to 30, and burns one unit of fuel per unit of throttle per second.

A throttle of 5 exactly cancels gravity and holds your speed. The gauge marks it `HOLD`.

## Landing

Touch down at 1 or less for a good landing. Between 1 and 5 the module survives with injuries. Above 5 nobody walks away.

The readout at the top right is the speed you will hit the ground at if you leave the throttle where it is. Green means a good landing, amber means injuries, red means a crash.

## The Trap

Every second you spend in the air costs 5 fuel just to stand still against gravity, so the tank is really a clock: about fourteen seconds of flight, however you spend it. Hovering to think is the most expensive thing you can do. Fall, then brake hard and late.
