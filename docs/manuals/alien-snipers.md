# Alien Snipers Manual

Your cruiser has strayed into a forbidden area with a damaged hyperspace motor, and sniper ships are jamming your radar. Every contact shows at the wrong place. Your computer knows the code.

## Controls

- `Left` / `Right`: choose the highest code number before the run.
- `Enter`: begin, and after a run, go again.
- `A` to `Z`: fire at that letter.
- `Esc`: return to the anthology landing page.

## The Code

You are given a false letter and a code number. Count that many letters on through the alphabet and fire at the one you land on.

For `M 4` fire at `Q`. For `C 2` fire at `E`.

## Rules

You get one shot per sniper and any letter you type takes it, so do not touch the keys until you have the answer. A wrong letter and running out of time cost you exactly the same: the sniper moves on.

There are ten snipers in a run.

## Difficulty

The number you choose before the run is the largest code number that can come up. Choosing a higher one also buys you more time per shot, so the setting is less punishing than it looks: from 2.5 seconds a shot at 1 up to 7 seconds at 10.

Your report at the end breaks your accuracy down by code number, which is the fastest way to find where your counting falls apart.
